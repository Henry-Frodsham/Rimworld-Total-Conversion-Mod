Trauma Team audio. Drop .ogg / .wav / .mp3 files into these folders and restart RimWorld:
  Landing/            AV landing (replaces the vanilla shuttle landing sound)
  Leaving/            AV take-off
  Callouts/Arrive     squad lands ("Trauma Team, clear the area!")
  Callouts/Contact    squad sees hostiles
  Callouts/Warning    a colonist walks into the exclusion zone
  Callouts/Secure     patient is aboard
  Callouts/Depart     squad boards to leave
Several files in one folder: one is picked at random each time. Empty folder: vanilla sound (Landing/Leaving) or silent (callouts).
Sound defs: Common/Defs/Sounds/Sounds_TraumaTeam.xml.
